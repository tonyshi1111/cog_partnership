Inbound & outbound flows

Inbound — partner approaches Cognition

  1. Lead capture
    Source: marketplace listing inquiry / website form / event meeting / customer overlap signal / warm intro. SLA: partnership team responds within 2 business days. Output: CRM lead with source attribution.
  2. Persona triage
    Which of 14 personas? Pre-disqualify: SI/channel partner (route to existing SI team); direct customer (route to sales). Out-of-scope: agent-neutral coexistence vendors (CodeRabbit / Greptile).
  3. Initial fit call (30 min)
    Partnership lead + partner BD lead. Agenda: their thesis on AI agents, customer pull for Devin / Windsurf, existing AI partner relationships. Exit criteria: worth a technical scoping call?
  4. Technical scoping (60 min)
    Partnership + Cognition product/eng + partner product/eng. Define integration surface (MCP-only / custom workflow / co-engineered), data flow, auth model. Exit: defined Tier 1/2/3 deal shape.
  5. Pilot scope agreement
    Written success criteria, timeline (4–8 weeks), shared customer (or simulated), measurement plan. Decision gate: proceed or kill (kill rate target <30%).
  6. Pilot build
    Eng work; partnership team owns PM. Internal stakeholders (sales, marketing, exec sponsor) kept in loop.
  7. Pilot success review
    Quantitative: success criteria met? Qualitative: partner enthusiasm, customer feedback. Decision gate: graduate to Tier 1 marketplace or Tier 2 featured-partner.
  8. Sales handoff → JOINT MOTION BEGINS
    Trigger: pilot success + partner identifies shared customer with budget. Partnership team intros partner BD to Cognition sales rep. RACI: partnership R for partner relationship; sales rep R for customer quota.
  9. Joint customer pursuit
    Sales-led, partnership-supported. Joint demo, proposal, reference architecture. Sales rep owns close.
  10. Co-marketing launch
    Joint blog, press release, conference co-presence, customer case study. Marketing owns execution; partnership owns content alignment.
  11. Renewal & expansion
    Quarterly partner business reviews. Watch for: new use cases, additional integrations, deal-shape graduation (Tier 1 → 2 → 3).

Outbound — Cognition pursues partner

  1. Target list per persona
    Top 3–5 named partners from deep-dives. Pre-disqualify: SI partners (other team); competitive lock-up (Snyk-Copilot, Snyk-Claude, Chainguard-Cursor). Track competitive urgency.
  2. Intel & approach planning
    Public partnership signals (announcements, blogs, conferences). Mutual customer overlap analysis. Decision-maker identification (BD lead, product lead, exec sponsor).
  3. Warm-intro stack
    • Layer 1: Cognition investor / advisor network (Founders Fund, Khosla, GIC)
    • Layer 2: Mutual customer references (Devin-using accounts who are partner customers)
    • Layer 3: Founder / exec personal network
    • Layer 4 (last resort): cold outreach
  4. Discovery call (30 min)
    Partnership lead pitches thesis + specific opportunity. Exit: do they understand why-Cognition-not-Cursor-or-Claude? Yes → continue.
  5. Joint roadmap proposal
    Written 1-pager: integration to build, customer story enabled, joint go-to-market motion, shared metric (revenue or activation). Iterate with partner BD.
  6. Pilot scope agreement
    Written success criteria, timeline, measurement plan (parallel to inbound step 5).
  7. Joint build
    Eng work; partnership owns PM and partner relationship.
  8. Sales handoff → JOINT MOTION BEGINS
    Same shape as inbound step 8 — partnership intros, sales owns quota.
  9. Joint customer pursuit
    Sales-led, partnership-supported.
  10. Co-marketing launch
    Joint blog, conference co-presence, customer case study.
  11. Renewal & expansion
    Quarterly partner business reviews; deal-shape graduation.

Sales / Partnership RACI matrix

Resolves the "murky middle" Ron called out. Partnership team owns the partner relationship and joint product / integration; sales owns the customer revenue.

Activity Partnership Sales Comments
Partner relationship management R/A I Partnership owns end-to-end
Joint integration / co-engineered features R/A C Partnership owns scope, eng executes
Marketplace listings & co-marketing R/A I Partnership owns content + launch
Revenue-share / strategic partner deals R/A C Partnership closes through
Customer revenue from partner-sourced leads C R/A Sales rep carries quota
Joint customer pursuit at shared customer C/S R/A Sales-led, partnership-supported (demo + content)
Partner Quarterly Business Review R/A C Partnership team runs
Termination / strategic divergence R/A I Partnership formalizes exit

R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed, S = Supports.

Proposed pipeline KPIs

Inbound metrics

  • Lead → first-call conversion rate (target: 60% within 2 weeks)
  • First-call → pilot conversion (target: 40%)
  • Pilot success rate (target: >70%, hard pre-qualification)
  • Time from inbound → signed pilot (target: 8 weeks)

Outbound metrics

  • Target accounts engaged per quarter (target: 5–8 per top-priority persona)
  • Warm-intro success rate (target: 50% of target accounts reached via warm path)
  • Discovery → pilot conversion (target: 25%)
  • Time from first contact → signed pilot (target: 16 weeks top-tier; 8 weeks opportunistic)

Joint motion metrics

  • Partner-sourced ARR (sales-tracked; partnership-attributed)
  • Customer wins per partnership in first 12 months (target: 3–5 flagship)
  • Co-marketing impressions / qualified leads per quarter
  • Net revenue retention on partnership-sourced accounts (target: >120%)

Strategic metrics

  • Tier 1 → Tier 2 graduations per year (target: 4–6)
  • Tier 2 → Tier 3 graduations per year (target: 1–2)
  • Competitive lock-out wins (we close before competitor does) — qualitative count

Worked examples

PagerDuty — Observability (Rank 2)
  1. Outbound start: PagerDuty on target list (top gap — Cursor + Claude have plugins, Devin doesn't).
  2. Intel: PagerDuty announced 30+ AI partners March 12, 2026 — actively partner-acquiring. ID their BD lead via LinkedIn + Anthropic-PagerDuty plugin docs.
  3. Warm intro: Cognition investor network → PagerDuty BD head (Layer 1).
  4. Discovery: Pitch the gap explicitly — "you have Cursor and Claude; the async / incident-triggered remediation lane is open for Devin."
  5. Joint roadmap: Devin as official "PagerDuty remediation agent" via MCP; one-click "send to Devin" button in PagerDuty UI on incident escalation.
  6. Pilot scope: 6-week pilot at mutual customer (e.g., Ramp uses both); success criteria = N incidents auto-remediated through Devin loop.
  7. Joint build: Engineering builds MCP + PagerDuty UI feature.
  8. Sales handoff: PagerDuty enterprise customers with Devin already → joint pursuit; sales reps engaged.
  9. Co-marketing: Joint blog + PagerDuty Summit co-presence (typically June / July).
  10. Renewal: Quarterly review with PagerDuty BD; expand to additional use cases (proactive change-management before incidents).
