Profile

Devin's COBOL case studies at Fortune 500 companies + Itaú's 5–6x migration speedup are real proof points.

But the competitive landscape just shifted dramatically: SAP Sapphire May 2026 announced a deepened SAP-Anthropic partnership making Claude the "primary reasoning and agentic capability across SAP's AI portfolio" via Joule. Anthropic now occupies the new-development lane at SAP. Cognition's lane narrows to migration TO SAP from legacy (where Devin's COBOL-to-modern strength is differentiated, distinct from Joule's forward-looking ERP work).

Value proposition

Cognition → migration platform

Solves the migration backlog the platform's enterprise customers won't tackle without an automation lever (these projects are 18–36 months with armies of consultants today). Faster migrations = more customers landing on the modern platform's recurring SaaS revenue.

Partner → Cognition

Enterprise customer distribution + co-sell with platform's enterprise sales reps + access to migration-budget line items (separate procurement category from typical software spend, often well-funded).
