Profile

Workflow and ticketing platforms are where engineering work is requested — Slack message, Jira ticket, Linear issue, Notion doc. An AI coding agent's most natural trigger surface is exactly this: an engineer messages "@devin investigate the latency regression in checkout-service" in Slack, or a PM files a Jira ticket and tags the engineering team.

Cognition has already shipped MCP integrations across all major surfaces (Slack, Jira, Linear, GitHub, GitLab in the Team tier at $500/month/seat). The partnership opportunity is now bimodal: deepen the existing relationships (Cognition is explicitly named in Slack Marketplace's leading-innovator group; Linear MCP is shipped) AND fill gaps in new partner-agent programs that emerged in early 2026 (Atlassian's "Agents in Jira" GA May 2026; Notion's Developer Platform GA May 13, 2026) where Devin is conspicuously absent from competitor lineups.
