Profile

Security tooling vendors detect vulnerabilities; coding agents fix them. This is one of the cleanest async loops in the entire Cognition portfolio — Ron explicitly flagged this category ("sneak or a sonar queue... we should be auto-remediating those automatically in an agentic way"). Every SAST / DAST / SCA scan produces issues that today sit in a backlog. With Devin as the remediation agent, the partnership economic shifts: security vendors get to claim end-to-end "detect-and-remediate" outcomes rather than detection-only — a much more compelling enterprise sale.

The competitive complication: the two best-known SAST vendors (Snyk and Chainguard) are already locked to other agents (Snyk-Copilot, Snyk-Claude May 8 2026, Chainguard-Cursor). Target selection becomes the work — go where the others haven't, or land a partnership at a vendor too important to be exclusive.

Where Cognition wins (2×2)

X-axis: Coverage of full vuln lifecycle (detect-only vs. detect → triage → fix → merge → verify). Y-axis: Enterprise scale. Competitor pairs (Snyk-Claude, Chainguard-Cursor) sit mid-right but lack Devin's async / cloud-sandbox execution model for closing the merge step.

Value proposition

Cognition → partner

Closes the remediation loop the security vendor can't close alone. Their detection becomes more valuable because it's coupled to fix-and-merge. Enables them to sell "outcome-based" pricing (detect-and-remediate) rather than tool-based pricing.

Partner → Cognition

Trigger surface + enterprise customer distribution + branding leverage. A "Wiz-certified" or "SonarQube-certified" remediation agent designation is strong enterprise trust signal. Wiz alone has thousands of cloud-native enterprise customers.

Deal shape

Pipeline notes

Inbound

Devin users already chain Snyk / Semgrep / SonarQube MCPs in their workflows → identify high-usage accounts where vuln-volume drives Devin usage → partnership team co-pitches to the security vendor.

Outbound

Target Wiz first (active partnership investment, Google Cloud connection, enterprise scale). Use Google Cloud relationship as a path-in. Then SonarQube (Ron's mention + open). Then Veracode for the strategic incumbent play.

Sales handoff

Security deals are joint enterprise sales — partnership team owns the security-vendor relationship + joint product; sales reps own the customer revenue when the security vendor co-sources the deal. Revenue share on Tier 3 bundled deals is partnership-team-owned through close.
