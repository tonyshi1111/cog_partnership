How the scoring works

Each persona scored 1–10 on three orthogonal dimensions; composite = (synergy + ease + market) / 3, equal-weighted. Full scoring framework on the Prioritization page.

Dimension What it measures
Synergy Product fit (how natively Cognition portfolio plugs into the partner workflow) + partner motivation + strategic moat / lock-out potential
Ease Existing relationship depth + engineering lift (MCP vs. custom build) + deal cycle / decision-maker accessibility + partner org complexity
Market Category TAM + flagship deal-size potential + number of credible named targets
